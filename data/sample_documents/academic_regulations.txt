Academic Regulations

Attendance Policy:
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for the semester examination. Students with attendance below 75% may be barred from the examination unless an approved exception applies.

Examination Policy:
Students must carry their valid college identity card to every examination. Mobile phones and other unauthorized electronic devices are prohibited in the examination hall.

Library Policy:
Students may borrow up to three books at a time. The standard borrowing period is 14 days. Overdue books may incur a fine according to college rules.

Academic Support:
Students who need academic assistance should contact their course instructor or academic advisor.
